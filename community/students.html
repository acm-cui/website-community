Introducing the ACM CUI Student Seminar 2027: Present, Listen, Connect

Conversational AI is moving fast, and some of its most exciting ideas are coming from early-stage researchers and undergraduate/graduate student researchers. The CUI Student Seminar is a new online series that brings that work to you, open to everyone around the world.

✨ One emerging researcher. One fresh paper. One hour of real conversation.

The seminar is hosted by the student community of ACM CUI, the SIGCHI-sponsored conference that has brought together researchers working on speech, chatbots, and conversational AI since 2018.

Present or listen

🎤 Want to present? If you're a PhD student, master's researcher, postdoc or early-stage researcher, get your work in front of peers worldwide, practice your talk in a friendly room, and walk away with fresh ideas for your next paper, deadline, or defense.

👂 Want to listen? Hear new research straight from the people who did it, ask about the choices and challenges that never make it into the paper, and meet others working on the same topics you are investigating.

Dates and deadlines

📅 Pilot season starts February 2027. Each session is one hour with a single presenter. The first presenters and attendees will help shape what the seminar becomes.

⏰ Presenter cohort calling deadline for February 2027: December 31, 2026 (AoE). Missed it? You can still submit afterwards or sign up for a later series.
